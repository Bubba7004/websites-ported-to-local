DELTARUNE · Chapter 1 · Field

Lancer fight

Lancer, the King's son, is the first boss of DELTARUNE Chapter 1, met out in the Field.

He rides at you on his bike, and with 540 HP the fight is short and forgiving: a good place to learn how the battle box, TP and ACTs work.

In the simulator you can play it straight from the fight list, practise one attack at a time, rewind a mistake, or let the autoplay SOUL show you a clean run.
