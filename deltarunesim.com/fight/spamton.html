Spamton fight

Spamton is the salesman hiding in a Cyber City alley in DELTARUNE Chapter 2.

His fight is short and loud: tiny copies of himself, flying words and a money vacuum. His secret fight, Spamton NEO, has its own page.

In the simulator you can play it straight from the fight list, practise one attack at a time, rewind a mistake, or let the autoplay SOUL show you a clean run.
