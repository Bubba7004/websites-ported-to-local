Pink fight

Pink is a DELTARUNE Chapter 5 boss with 7,000 HP and a lot of flirting: most of the ACTs are flirts of growing strength.

The attacks run from cats and bombs to a rotating box and a 3D tunnel.

In the simulator you can play it straight from the fight list, practise one attack at a time, rewind a mistake, or let the autoplay SOUL show you a clean run.
