Muffet fight

Muffet runs the spider bake sale, and she is the boss you meet in her web in UNDERTALE's Hotland.

Your SOUL turns purple and is stuck on three strings while spiders and her cupcake pet close in.

In the simulator you can play it straight from the fight list, practise one attack at a time, rewind a mistake, or let the autoplay SOUL show you a clean run.
