K. Round fight

K. Round, the crowned checker piece, is a miniboss of DELTARUNE Chapter 1.

Its leaps across the box are easy to read but hit hard, and Susie has an ACT of her own to help bring it down.

In the simulator you can play it straight from the fight list, practise one attack at a time, rewind a mistake, or let the autoplay SOUL show you a clean run.
