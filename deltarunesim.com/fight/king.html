DELTARUNE · Chapter 1 · Card Castle

King fight

King is the final boss of DELTARUNE Chapter 1, fought at the top of Card Castle.

He fills the box with chains, falling spades and bouncing boxes, and he has 2,800 HP, so expect a long fight.

In the simulator you can play it straight from the fight list, practise one attack at a time, rewind a mistake, or let the autoplay SOUL show you a clean run.
