Mettaton NEO fight

Mettaton NEO is Mettaton's genocide-route form in UNDERTALE, waiting at the end of the Core.

It is famous for how the fight ends, and the simulator plays it exactly as the game does.

In the simulator you can play it straight from the fight list, practise one attack at a time, rewind a mistake, or let the autoplay SOUL show you a clean run.
