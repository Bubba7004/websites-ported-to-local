Hammer of Justice fight

The Hammer of Justice is one of DELTARUNE Chapter 4's secret bosses.

It is a fast, aggressive fight that tests how well you read an attack the first time you see it.

In the simulator you can play it straight from the fight list, practise one attack at a time, rewind a mistake, or let the autoplay SOUL show you a clean run.
