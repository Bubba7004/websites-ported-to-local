Papyrus fight

Papyrus, the skeleton who wants to capture a human, is UNDERTALE's Snowdin boss.

He turns your SOUL blue, so gravity pulls it down and you jump over his bones, pattern after pattern.

In the simulator you can play it straight from the fight list, practise one attack at a time, rewind a mistake, or let the autoplay SOUL show you a clean run.
