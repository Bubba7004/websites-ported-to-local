Photoshop Flowey fight

After Asgore on the neutral route, Flowey takes the six human SOULs and becomes Photoshop Flowey, the strangest fight in UNDERTALE.

The screen fills with vines, lasers and missiles, and the SOULs inside him change the fight as it goes.

In the simulator you can play it straight from the fight list, practise one attack at a time, rewind a mistake, or let the autoplay SOUL show you a clean run.
