Titan fight

The Titan is the final boss of DELTARUNE Chapter 4: "the fear of dark", as its CHECK puts it, with 21,000 HP.

Dark shapes and shape centipedes fill the screen, and the party has ACTs of its own to push the darkness back.

In the simulator you can play it straight from the fight list, practise one attack at a time, rewind a mistake, or let the autoplay SOUL show you a clean run.
